Engineering / data limitation

ARM curves were manually estimated from screenshot photographs and may contain digitization errors or inconsistencies. The two independent fuel estimates can differ because SFOC is ISO-corrected, chart conditions vary, and Peff vs Pd may not be identical. MCR=26,900 kW is inherited from the original v1.1 app; the photograph appears to top out around 26,000 kW. Confirm manufacturer MCR and ARM parameter definitions. Never adjust real records solely to match reference curves.
